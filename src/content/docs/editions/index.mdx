# Editions

XrmGhost is presented publicly in two editions so teams can understand the product path without reading internal packaging detail.

## Trial

Trial is the entry point for evaluation and early hands-on learning.

Use Trial when you want to:

- understand the local-first workflow
- validate that XrmGhost fits your Dataverse plugin development process
- explore the developer experience before broader adoption

Trial is intended to help teams confirm product fit and learn the shape of the workflow.

## Standard

Standard is the primary edition for ongoing team usage once evaluation is complete.

Use Standard when you want to:

- adopt XrmGhost as part of a regular plugin development workflow
- support repeatable local execution across a team
- move beyond evaluation into sustained use

Standard represents the production-oriented path for teams that are ready to operationalize the workflow.

## Choosing a starting point

| If your goal is... | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| Evaluate the product and learn the workflow | Trial |
| Use XrmGhost as a regular team capability | Standard |
