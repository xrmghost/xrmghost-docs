# Start a Discussion

Use discussions for open-ended topics that are not ready to become bug reports or feature requests.

Open a discussion in the public repository: [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/xrmghost/xrmghost/discussions)

## Good Use Cases

- You want feedback before filing a formal feature request.
- You are comparing alternative approaches and want community input.
- You have a how-to question that could help other users later.
- You want to share experiments, patterns, or lessons learned.

## Discussion vs Issue

- Open an **issue** when action is clear and trackable (bug fix or concrete feature).
- Open a **discussion** when the topic is exploratory, broad, or still being shaped.

If a discussion converges on a concrete action, maintainers can convert or link it to an issue.

## What to Include

- Context about your scenario
- What you already tried
- What decision or feedback you are looking for
