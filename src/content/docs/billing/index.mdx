# Billing and pricing

This page explains how changes to an XrmGhost subscription are billed, how promotions apply, and the terms of the current annual launch campaign. The binding terms are the XrmGhost Terms of Service; this page describes their commercial effect in plain language.

## Proration

When a change to your subscription is prorated, the prorated amount is **calculated by Stripe**, XrmGhost's payment processor, and shown to you in the checkout or plan-change flow before you confirm. XrmGhost does not calculate prorated amounts itself and does not issue manual refunds or credits for plan changes.

A billing customer has **one active subscription at a time**: a billing customer cannot have two active Stripe subscriptions for the same customer. Changing plan, seats, or billing cadence modifies that single subscription rather than adding a second one.

## Billing-change matrix

| Change | When it takes effect | What is billed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plan increase (upgrade to a higher plan) | Immediately | Prorated charge for the rest of the current billing period, calculated by Stripe |
| Seat increase (adding seats) | Immediately | Prorated charge for the added seats for the rest of the current billing period, calculated by Stripe |
| Plan decrease (downgrade to a lower plan) | Scheduled for the end of the current billing period | You keep the current plan until period end; the lower plan is billed from the next period |
| Seat removal | At the end of the current billing period | No credit for the removed seats; the lower seat count is billed from the next period |
| Monthly → annual | Immediately, and a new annual billing cycle starts on the day of the change | Prorated charge, calculated by Stripe |
| Annual → monthly | Scheduled for the end of the current annual term | No credit; monthly billing starts when the annual term ends |

In short: **increases are immediate and prorated; decreases wait for the end of the period.** Apart from the prorated adjustments described above, fees already paid are not refunded or credited for partial periods, unused time, downgrades, or cancellations.

## Promotions

Promotional discounts follow the same rules for every campaign:

- **First period only.** A promotional discount applies to the first billing period of the subscription it is redeemed on.
- **Renewal at list price.** From the first renewal onward, the subscription renews at the then-current list price.
- **Not stackable.** Only one promotion applies to a purchase. If more than one promotion is available, the **larger discount wins**; if two discounts are equal, the **more recent campaign wins**.
- **One redemption per billing customer.** Each billing customer can redeem a given promotion once.
